# Future testing and acceptance

All results are pending implementation. The following criteria are proposed for a controlled educational environment.

Future tabletop verifies completeness from context through ongoing monitoring. Require one owner and review date for each risk, an evidence plan for every proposed control, documented assumptions for scores, and approved residual decisions. Two reviewers independently score the same fictional scenarios and reconcile differences of more than one ordinal step. Exercise an outage and access incident with timed escalation and dependency logs. Verify RTO/RPO definitions through the continuity template and record remaining unknowns.

## Control checks

| Control objective | Check and expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier outage | Tabletop tests alternate intake and restore evidence |
| Account takeover | Review fictional coverage denominator and escalation ownership |
| Ransomware impact | Tabletop links assumptions and owner to decision |
| Bias or tampering | Changes have rationale, timestamp and signer |

## Evidence and disposition

Use the [structured evidence template](../templates/risk-register.md). Record fixture or revision, operator, expected and observed outcomes, timestamps, and supporting evidence. Use pass, fail, blocked or unexecuted and preserve the first result on retest.
